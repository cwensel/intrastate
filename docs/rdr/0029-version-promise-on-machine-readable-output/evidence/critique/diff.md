## Contradictions between the passes

Two, both on `0029:A3`/`planEnvelope`, and they are contradictions of MECHANISM rather than
of fact — neither pass asserts something the other denies, but each asserts a different thing
is the real defect, and only one can be the primary.

**1. What makes A3's verification insufficient.**
B (C-10, and B's §3 pick) says A3 is Verified "against a population of one" and that the real
risk is external consumers who are categorically unauditable — the verification method is
unsound because the population is wrong.
A (C-2, second arm) says the citation does not even cover the right code inside that one
consumer: `0029:A3` and `0029:S3` read `planEnvelope`'s struct definition and stop, never
reaching `readPlan`'s discrimination at `internal/cli/flow_input.go:414-415`.

**Winner: A, on the cite.** `0029:A3`'s Evidence is verbatim "a four-field struct read by a
plain `json.Unmarshal`, which ignores unrecognized top-level properties by Go's default
decoder behaviour" — the decode, and nothing past it. A's claim is checkable and checks out.
B's claim is true but unfalsifiable by construction (you cannot audit an unenumerable
population), which is why the RDR's own answer to it — land during `0.x` — is a reasonable
one. B's row is not wrong; it is the weaker of two true things about the same passage.
Both go in the consolidated ledger, A's as the higher-severity row.

**2. Whether the RDR states the consumer's discriminator correctly.**
A (C-2) says the RDR names `schema_version` as the discriminator and that this is false.
B never engages the discriminator question at all, and B's C-1/C-6 implicitly treat C1's
consumer rules as coherent-but-unenforced.

**Winner: A.** `0029:§illustrative-code` carries both sentences. And `0029:§activation-step-1`
item 3 states the CORRECT rule — "a consumer branches on the presence of `code`, not on
`type`" — and calls it "the single most consumer-relevant fact in the record", which means
the RDR holds the right rule in one section and the wrong one in another. B's framing, which
assumes C1's consumer rules are internally consistent, does not survive that cite.

No contradiction found where both passes assert incompatible FACTS about the tree. Every
code claim either pass makes that was checked against `internal/` holds.

# Charted to successor — critique iteration 1

Charted: **C-14 contract count vs. the `foundational — one contract` Profile.**
The critique counts at least five independently load-bearing contracts in this
RDR's Normative Contracts — the atom domain rule, strong-Kleene combination,
provenance-blind presence, the resolution-level aggregation veto, and the
`RequiresOwned` redefinition — each with distinct consumers (0003's evaluator,
the kernel, 0002's authors) and distinct failure modes, against a Metadata
`Profile` claiming one contract and a Proportionality gate whose split signal is
"sole author of at most one independent load-bearing contract".

Why not resolved in this loop: the split test is the **Finalization Gate's**
Proportionality item, which is written to be answered at Stage 7 with the
contracts counted in front of the author — and the gate section is still an
unfilled template. Acting on it here would mean either (a) rewriting the Profile
field on a lens's authority, or (b) splitting the RDR, which is net-new scope of
the largest kind and would strand A9/A10/A12/A13 mid-verification across two
documents. Neither is a pre-lock resolve's call.

The counter-argument the gate must weigh: the five clauses arguably decompose one
contract — "what the evaluator answers over an incomplete view" — into its
operator-level, combination-level, and resolution-level statements, which is the
shape a single domain rule takes when its seam spans two peers. The
`RequiresOwned` redefinition is the weakest fit for that framing and is the most
plausible split candidate if the gate finds one.

Suggested successor: **`/rdr-finalize 0007` Proportionality response** — count the
contracts, then either justify the `foundational — one contract` value in writing
or correct the field and split before locking. If it splits, the
`RequiresOwned` narrowing is the seam to lift out.
